    How to Play

  • 1. Take Turns: The first player places their symbol (X or O) in any empty square.

  • 2. Alternate: Players alternate turns, placing their symbol in one remaining open square.

  • 3. Attack and Defend: Try to get three of your symbols in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line while blocking your opponent from doing the same.
